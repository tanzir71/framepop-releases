FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need design or animation experience?+

No. Drop in your screenshots, choose a template, and export. The motion and timing are already designed.

Does my work get uploaded anywhere?+

No. FramePop has no upload step, cloud renderer, account, or telemetry. Your images and video remain on your Mac.

How is this different from After Effects?+

FramePop is purpose-built for UI presentation videos. It gets you to a polished export in a minute, not an afternoon of timelines and keyframes.

How is this different from Rotato?+

Rotato specializes in 3D device mockups. FramePop specializes in template-driven motion for flat screenshots, with content-aware layout that keeps every screen readable.

Do I need a subscription?+

No. FramePop is a one-time purchase.

What macOS version do I need?+

macOS 14 or newer on Apple Silicon.

Is there a Windows version?+

Not yet. FramePop is currently built specifically for macOS.

Can I use exports commercially?+

Yes. Commercial use is included.

What file types can I drop in?+

PNG, JPG, WebP, and AVIF.

Can I use my own background?+

Yes. Use any image as a background, or choose from the built-in solid and gradient collection.
