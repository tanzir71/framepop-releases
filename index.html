Questions

Do I need motion design experience?

No. Drop in your screens and pick a look. Timing, easing and transitions are already designed; you can still change any of them.

Is anything uploaded?

No. FramePop has no account, upload step, cloud renderer or telemetry. Your screens and exports stay on your computer.

Can I reorder screens after picking a look?

Yes. Drag cards in the storyboard or the Arrange grid, or use the keyboard. Transitions re-link to their new neighbours and the preview follows.

Which file types can I drop in?

PNG, JPG, WebP and AVIF screenshots, desktop or mobile, any height.

Which systems are supported?

macOS 14 or newer on Apple silicon, and Windows 10 22H2 or newer on x64.

Can I use exports commercially?

Yes. Commercial use is included.
